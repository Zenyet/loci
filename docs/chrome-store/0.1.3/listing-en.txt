Detailed description (copy from the next line)
Bring your saved pages back into view.

Loci turns your Chrome new tab into a bookmark graph. Explore folders, search for a website, and save pages to an inbox to organize later.

Explore and organize
• Pan and zoom through your bookmarks and folders.
• Open a folder to explore its contents, or click a bookmark to visit it.
• Search titles, URLs and groups. Use Ctrl / Cmd + K to open search.
• Create, edit and move bookmarks using Chrome's own bookmark system.
• Access frequent sites and recent visits from the new tab.

Save now, organize later
• Use the Loci toolbar button to save the current page.
• Collect pages in an inbox, then move them into folders.
• Optionally connect your own AI provider and API key for suggested organization. Review the plan before applying it.

Make room for everyday tools
• A calendar with lunar dates and the included 2026 mainland China holiday schedule.
• Local notes and tasks, with a way to turn a note into a task.
• A focus timer that can be linked to a task.
• Bookmark rediscovery from your existing collection.
• Optional weather with manual city selection or a location request you initiate.
• Optional mainland China market snapshots using your own Tushare connection.
• Background, appearance and widget settings, with up to three pinned tools.

Local-first, optional connections
Core bookmark features do not require a Loci account. Settings, tasks, notes and timer state stay on your device. Bookmarks remain in Chrome's bookmark system; Chrome account settings control bookmark sync.

AI, weather and market data are optional. AI requires your own service configuration and API key; requested operations send relevant bookmark titles, domains and organization data to that provider. Weather uses Open-Meteo, and an explicit location request uses BigDataCloud. Market data requires your own Tushare Token and the appropriate endpoint access. Third-party services may have separate quotas or fees. Loci does not execute securities trades.

The interface is currently primarily in Simplified Chinese. Listing screenshots use sample bookmarks and manually enabled widgets.
